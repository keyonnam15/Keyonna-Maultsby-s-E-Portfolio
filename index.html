Leveling Up Learning Through Technology

Welcome to my EME 6208 Digital Portfolio. I am an educator serving students in grades 5-9 mathematics with a passion for technology and creativity. This portfolio showcases projects that I developed throughout this course. My goal is to combine educational practices and technology to ultimately create differentiated opportunities for students to achieve math proficiency.

Image Editing

Project 1 required the creation of a book cover. My book cover, titled "Through the Storm" is inspired by the steadfastness Jesus portrayed during bad storms and seasons. The first image was traditionally created with Adobe Photoshop, and the second image was created with AI tools built into Adobe Creative Cloud.
